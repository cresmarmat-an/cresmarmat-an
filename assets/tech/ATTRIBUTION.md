## Rojo

The Rojo wordmark is the vector artwork from:

https://github.com/rojo-rbx/rojo/blob/master/assets/images/header-logo.svg

The complete vector geometry is embedded in `rojo.svg`. Only its placement and scale changed. The Rojo source is distributed under Mozilla Public License 2.0, included in [LICENSE-ROJO.txt](./LICENSE-ROJO.txt). The modified SVG source is distributed here in editable form.

## Presentation changes

Each SVG is embedded directly with no network dependencies. A navy background, border, and text label were added. Rust is rendered in a pale foreground color for contrast. Lua retains its original colors and sits on a pale circular backing. Other logo geometry and colors are retained.
